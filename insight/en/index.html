WHAT'S INSIDE

One quarter, seen through four lenses

The same revenue reads differently once you place it on top of industry trends, trade-area data, and tax law.

  • 01

    Financial highlights

    Monthly P&L closed in your bookkeeping software — an accountant flags the trend and anomalies in revenue, cost ratio, and labor ratio.

  • 02

    Industry benchmark

    We read the direction of your sector from the results of listed peers filed on DART. The numbers speak before the news does.

  • 03

    Trade-area signals

    Using Seoul commercial-district data, we track your area's sales, store count, foot traffic, and time-of-day spending every quarter.

  • 04

    Tax-saving diagnosis

    We match tax-incentive reductions and credits against your situation to find what you're missing — re-checked each quarter, reflecting amendments and sunset extensions.

Why quarterly? Both DART filings and Seoul trade-area data refresh quarterly. We go deep only when the data is new — we don't produce a report that repeats itself every month. Meanwhile, each month's numbers are checked quickly and lightly via the monthly closing snapshot.
HOW IT WORKS
